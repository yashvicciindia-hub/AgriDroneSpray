Who we are

Built by people who know Indian fields, not just drones.

We're a regional drone spraying operation working directly with farmers, FPOs and Custom Hiring Centres. We started as a single-district pilot and are building toward a franchised network of operators across the country — every hub trained, insured, and fully compliant with DGCA rules.
