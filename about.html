Our mission

Modern drone spraying for a smarter, more resilient farm economy.

We bring together certified pilots, precision field planning, and dependable seasonal support to help growers get more consistent results with less waste.

Why we exist

Indian agriculture needs more dependable crop-care services that are faster, safer, and more measurable than traditional methods. AgriDroneSpray was built to make drone-based operations accessible to farmers, FPOs, and custom hiring networks without requiring the farmer to own expensive equipment.

How we work

We pair field registration, scheduling, pilot assignment, and documentation into one service experience. Our team handles the practical realities of compliance, safety, and reporting while farmers focus on crop performance.

What makes our model practical

DGCAcompliant operations
NPNTregistered flights
1-stepfield booking

Our operating principles

  • Prioritize safe, measured application over rushed coverage.
  • Keep the farmer experience simple, with booking and reporting in one flow.
  • Support local field teams and partners so the service scales with real regional needs.
  • Use documented, accountable workflows that are easier to trust and easier to repeat.
