How it works

A simple process from first registration to field completion.

We keep the workflow clear and practical so every farm gets a consistent experience, from planning to final reporting.

1

Register your farm

Tell us the location, crop, field size and service requirement. This helps our team plan the correct route and timing for your field.

2

We schedule your slot

Once we review your details, we coordinate dates and confirm the spray slot based on crop stage, weather and crew availability.

3

We spray the field

A certified remote pilot flies the drone using an approved operating plan so the field is covered evenly and safely.

4

You receive the report

We provide documentation for the flight, chemical batch and service details so the work remains traceable and accountable.

Why this process matters

A reliable crop-care workflow should reduce uncertainty, not create more. Our process is intentionally practical: clear owner communication, documented pilot operation, and a final report that helps growers understand what happened in the field.
